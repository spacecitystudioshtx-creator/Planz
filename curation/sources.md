# Planz curated sources (living list)

This is the list of places Planz trusts to find things to do. **It's a living document.** Claude updates it on every curation run, and you can edit it by hand anytime.

## How a source gets on this list
1. **Vouched by friends.** A friend sends their "last 10 cool things I did" (see `inbox/`). Claude works out where each one was announced (a newsletter, an Instagram account, a venue calendar, a listings site) and adds that place here with the evidence. Each additional friend's item found there adds +1 to its **Vouches** count.
2. **Seed.** A starting set Claude picked before any friend lists came in. Seeds with 0 vouches drop a tier after 4 weeks if their picks keep getting cut or swiped left.

## Tiers
- **A: trusted curators.** These are the main source. An event mentioned here starts with a high score.
- **B: trusted venues, promoters and accounts.** Read their calendars directly.
- **C: directories.** Eventbrite, Ticketmaster and similar. Only used for specific gaps (singles events, big-name shows), always with strict filters.

Promotion rules: **3+ vouches → Tier A.** **1–2 vouches → at least Tier B.** A source whose picks make the final cut less than 10% of the time for 4 runs → drop a tier.

## Fields per source
`Tier` · `URL` · `Covers` · `Vouches` (number, plus who sent each item) · `Evidence` (the friend items found there) · `Last run` (candidates found / made the cut) · `Notes`
