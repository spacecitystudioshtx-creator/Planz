# Planz curation rubric

Planz's whole value is showing **a few discrete, cool ideas, not a pile of listings**. Every candidate event is judged against this guide. When in doubt, cut it.

## 1. Hard cuts (drop immediately)
- Generic bar promos: happy hours, "bottomless brunch party", "day party", pub crawls, bar-hop passes
- Networking mixers, "founders meetups", real-estate or crypto talks, MLM, sales pitches dressed as workshops
- Online or virtual-only events
- No named artist, host, chef, curator or specific thing ("Live music Friday!", "DJ night")
- Promoter-spam patterns: the same organizer posting many near-identical listings, "VIP table" pricing, "guest list" upsells
- Outside the area: not in Manhattan, Brooklyn or Queens, and not a train-ride big trip
- No official link we can send people to
- Sold out with no waitlist or door option (unless it's a recurring thing that's worth saving)

## 2. Score (0–100)

| Signal | Points | How to judge |
|---|---|---|
| **Curator mentions** | up to 30 | Tier A mention = 15 each (max 30). Tier B = 8 each. Vouched sources (friends) count double. |
| **Distinctive** | up to 20 | One-time or rare (20) > limited run (12) > a great weekly institution (8) > generic recurring (0) |
| **Specific and notable** | up to 15 | A named act or host with real following, an acclaimed show, a known chef or artist, a famous venue |
| **Group-chat test** | up to 15 | "Would you text a friend about this?" Novelty, story value, a sense of "only in NYC" |
| **Worth the effort** | up to 10 | Good value for the price and travel. Free and great = 10. |
| **Matches friend taste** | up to 10 | Similar to "loved" items in `examples.md`. Similar to "skipped" items = subtract up to 10. |

**The cutoff is 55.** Anything below it doesn't ship.

## 3. The "why go" line (required)
Write one specific sentence a friend would say, 90 characters max.
- Good: "Only NYC date of their reunion tour, in a 500-cap room."
- Good: "The Brooklyn Museum stays open late, free, with a DJ in the lobby."
- Bad: "A fun night out with great music!" (generic, so **cut the event**)

**If you can't write a specific why-go line, the event is cut.**

## 4. Balance and cap (after scoring)
- Ship **at most 50 events** per run, covering the next 14 days. The top 10 by score are marked `pick: true` (Editor's Pick).
- No single category more than 30% of the list.
- At least 5 free events, and something in each of Manhattan, Brooklyn and Queens.
- At least 2 big trips (train-ride away) and at least 3 weekly institutions worth saving.
- Each weekend day (Fri, Sat, Sun) has at least 8 options.
- If there aren't enough good events, **ship fewer**. Never pad the list with junk.

## 5. Concerts
- The artist must be named and notable enough to be worth a ticket: curator coverage, a real following, or a genuinely buzzy small act.
- Tag the genre precisely in `sub` (Indie & Rock, Electronic, Jazz, Hip-Hop & R&B, Classical, Singer-Songwriter) so the app's taste matching works.
- "A bar with a band on Friday" is not a concert listing.

## 6. Honesty
- Every date, price and link must come from a real source found during the run. Never guess a date.
- If the price is unknown, write "See site". If the time is unknown, use the venue's usual door time and note it in `tagline`.
